About Me

Passionate about building functional software through continuous hands-on learning.

I'm Andrew Llaneta (20 y/o), currently enrolled as a 3rd Year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at the National College of Science and Technology (NCST) in Dasmariñas, Cavite. METHODOLOGY

My Development Process

A structured workflow I follow when approaching software challenges and interface design.

Phase 01

UNDERSTAND

Analyze the problem, research use cases, and outline clear project requirements.

Phase 02

Phase 03

DESIGN

Phase 04
